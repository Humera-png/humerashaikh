Why I Built LeadFlow

When I started my automation architect business, I faced a challenge: how do I prove to clients that I can build exactly what I recommend? How do I show them that I practice what I preach?

The answer was LeadFlow. I decided to build my own custom lead management and automation tool to solve my own problem while simultaneously proving my technical expertise.

The Challenge

  • Managing 44+ leads manually across spreadsheets
  • Following up inconsistently (human memory fails)
  • No visibility into lead status in real-time
  • Spending 10+ hours per week on lead management
  • Couldn't demonstrate automation expertise to prospects

The Solution

I built LeadFlow from scratch—a custom full-stack automation dashboard that manages my entire lead pipeline and runs on autopilot. The tool demonstrates three critical capabilities: full-stack development skills, automation workflow design, and business process optimization.

What LeadFlow Does

  • Centralized lead pipeline (44+ leads in one place)
  • Automated email sequences (Day 1, Day 5, Day 10)
  • Real-time open and reply tracking
  • Calendar integration for call booking
  • Status tracking (Cold to Warm to Hot to Customer)
  • Automation engine that runs 24/7 with zero manual input
